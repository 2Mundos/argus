Docs-only runbook for tasks stuck `ready` after execution chains are configured.

Incident: after the operator saved execution chains in Setup for difficulties
of the configured difficulties, new tasks of those difficulties stayed `ready`
forever and `POST /api/tasks/claim` kept returning 204, with nothing explaining
why in the task view.

Cause: once any difficulty has a saved chain, a worker is admitted for that
route only if its pool capabilities — rendered into `ARGUS_CAPABILITIES` —
contain `execution-chains-v1` plus the `nanoclaw:harness:<adapter>:<provider>`
and `nanoclaw:executor:<adapter>:<provider>` capability IDs of the route.
A worker that declares none keeps legacy routing and cannot claim
chain-routed tasks.

Fix: PATCH the pool `capabilities` with `execution-chains-v1` and the exact
adapter capability IDs for the adapters the agent image really contains (probe
the CLIs in the image first), then recycle idle instances one at a time; new
instances inherit the corrected declaration at start, and never recycle an
instance that holds a claimed task.

Also documents: the separate provider-side symptom (`engine_account_unavailable`
/ `awaiting_execution_route`, `max_leases` vs `active_leases` for the shared
`local` account — report to the owner rather than raising leases blindly), and
the last line that after a host restart a task attempt's lease expires and the
task is requeued (steers to the dead attempt are lost).
