# Account auth health: presence is not validity

Status: implemented in [account usage](../specs/account-usage.spec.md) 3.5 and 3.6 · 2026-10-29

## Context

An Argus account is `available`, `exhausted` or `disabled`
([account selection](../specs/account-selection.spec.md)). For
[Claude](../specs/claude-accounts.spec.md) Argus can read the credential's
`expiresAt`; for [Codex](../specs/codex-accounts.spec.md) a structured
`auth_expired` notice marks the account exhausted with a fixed reason. Neither
answers the operator's real question: did this account last work, and when?

[Cloudroom](https://github.com/davidondrej/cloudroom) (`core/docs/observability.md`,
"Claude login monitoring") records two independent facts per login:

- `credentials`: `unknown`, `present`, `missing` or `check_failed`, from a local,
  free status check.
- `last_request`: `unknown`, `accepted` or `rejected`, with a timestamp. Only a
  completed inference with output tokens counts as accepted; only the native
  authentication-failure code counts as rejected. Rate limits and unrelated
  failures change neither.

Their finding is that Claude Code reports expired synthetic credentials as logged
in, so presence alone proves nothing.

## Proposal

Show and expose, per model account, the two facts separately:

- **Credential state**, derived on read from what Argus stores: present, missing,
  or expired (Claude `expiresAt` in the past). It is never shown as "healthy".
- **Last request**: `last_accepted_at` from the usage relay (an attempt that
  reported output tokens on this account), and `last_rejected_at` with its reason
  from auth-failure notices (Codex `auth_expired`, and the Claude equivalent once
  identified). Rate limits and quota notices do not touch either field.

The accounts table and Home's attention list flag an account whose last rejection
is newer than its last acceptance. No active probing: no paid call, and no
background `claude auth status`, because credentials are not on a worker until
leased.

## What Argus has today

Exhaustion marks with reasons, usage per account
([account usage](../specs/account-usage.spec.md)), and the Codex quota probe.
There is no recorded "last accepted" or "last rejected" per account.

## Success

After a forced auth failure on a disposable account, the dashboard shows it
rejected at that time, while a sibling account that just completed a task shows
accepted. A rate-limited account shows neither.

## Open questions

- Can `last_accepted_at` be derived on read from existing usage rows, or does it
  need a column (and therefore a migration)?
- What is Claude Code's stable authentication-failure signal in stream JSON?
  Cloudroom uses `authentication_failed`; verify against the CLI version in the
  engines layer.

## Out of scope

Automatic credential renewal (native CLIs own it), and disabling accounts
automatically on rejection.
