# Harness stderr tail on failed attempts

Status: draft · 2026-10-29

## Context

When a session container vanishes, the NanoClaw channel fails the attempt as
`session_lost` with an error that ends in `last output: …`. That is the
container's combined log, redacted and capped at 2 KiB
(`SESSION_LOG_TAIL_BYTES` in `integrations/nanoclaw/argus.ts`). The tail lives
only inside the error string. Failures where the session never started, or exits
that are not `session_lost`, keep no tail at all. The
[task live log](../specs/task-live-log.spec.md) is a ring of model-facing output,
not the harness's own stderr.

[Cloudroom](https://github.com/davidondrej/cloudroom) (`core/docs/observability.md`,
"Protected harness stderr") treats harness stderr as untrusted and sensitive:

- It drains stderr continuously and keeps the last 16 KiB in memory.
- It saves one tail per exit, including startup and resume failures.
- It flags `stderr_truncated` and `stderr_complete: false` so a reader knows
  what is missing.
- It keeps the tail out of ordinary logs, in owner-only files with size-based
  rotation.

## Proposal

- **Capture the harness's stderr separately** from model output, bounded by bytes
  (16 KiB in memory), on every exit: completion, failure, `session_lost`,
  startup failure.
- **Attach it to the attempt** as a small artifact (`stderr-tail`), redacted
  with the same redaction as the live log, with `truncated` and `complete`
  flags. The `session_lost` error keeps a short pointer instead of the inline
  text.
- **Readable under existing rules.** Admins and orchestrators can read it;
  workers only for tasks they hold
  ([worker read scope](../specs/worker-read-scope.spec.md)). It never goes to
  Prometheus labels, events, or the live feed.
- **Bounded on disk**: one tail per attempt, artifact size capped, removed with
  the task's other artifacts.

## Success

A harness that crashes on startup with a stack trace leaves a readable, redacted
tail on its attempt; an attempt that ends normally keeps one only if stderr was
nonempty; a secret printed to stderr in a fixture appears redacted.

## Open questions

- Can NanoClaw separate the harness's stderr from the container's stdout, or
  does Argus only see `docker logs`? If only combined logs are available, the
  improvement is the artifact and the flags, not the separation.
- Is 16 KiB the right cap for Codex and Claude crash traces in practice?

## Out of scope

Shipping stderr anywhere off-node, and treating stderr as evidence of model
behaviour.
